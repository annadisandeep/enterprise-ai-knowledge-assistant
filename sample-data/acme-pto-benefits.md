# Acme Corp Paid Time Off and Benefits

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year during their first
three years of service. From the fourth year onward, employees accrue 20 days per
year. Unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following calendar
year; any excess is forfeited on 31 December.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick days do not
carry over and are not paid out on termination.

## Parental Leave

Acme offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents, which must
be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

## Health Benefits

Acme covers 90 percent of the premium for employee medical, dental, and vision
insurance, and 70 percent of the premium for dependents. Coverage begins on the
first day of the month following the start date.

## Retirement

Acme matches employee 401(k) contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 4 percent of
base salary. Matching contributions vest immediately.
