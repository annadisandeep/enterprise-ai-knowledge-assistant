Acme Corp Information Security Handbook

Password Policy
Employees must use passwords of at least 14 characters that include a mix of
upper and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be rotated
every 180 days. Reusing any of the previous five passwords is prohibited. All
accounts that support it must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).

Data Classification
Acme classifies data into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and
Restricted. Restricted data includes customer payment information and must be
encrypted both at rest and in transit. Only employees with a documented business
need may access Restricted data.

Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Operations
Center within one hour of discovery by emailing security@acme.example or calling
the 24/7 hotline. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate an incident on your
own unless instructed by the Security team.

Acceptable Use
Company systems may be used for incidental personal use that does not interfere
with work, consume significant resources, or violate any law or policy. Installing
unapproved software on company devices is prohibited.

Phishing
Never click links or open attachments from unexpected emails. Report suspected
phishing using the "Report Phishing" button in the email client. Acme will never
ask for your password by email or phone.
