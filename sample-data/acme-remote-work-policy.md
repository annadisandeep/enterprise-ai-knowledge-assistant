# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period are
eligible to work remotely up to three days per week. New hires must work on-site
during their first 90 days so they can complete security training and meet their
teams.

## Equipment

Acme provides every remote employee with a company laptop, an external monitor,
and a stipend of 300 US dollars for home-office furniture. Employees are
responsible for providing their own reliable internet connection of at least
50 Mbps.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must connect through the Acme VPN whenever they access internal
systems. Company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatic
screen lock set to five minutes. Personal devices may never be used to store
customer data.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, employees are expected to be available during core hours
of 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within
core hours whenever possible.

## Expense Reimbursement

Home internet is not reimbursed. However, employees may expense co-working space
day passes up to 25 US dollars per day, for a maximum of five days per month,
with manager approval.
